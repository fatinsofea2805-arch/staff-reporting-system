About Pasir Mas Land District

The Land District of Pasir Mas, located in the state of Kelantan, Malaysia, is dedicated to providing efficient land administration and public service. We aim to serve our community through accessible services and a commitment to transparency.

Services

Land Survey

Providing professional land survey services for accurate property mapping and registration.

Document Processing

Assisting with land document registration, title issuance, and updates for property owners.

Community Services

We are committed to supporting the local community with services that enhance quality of life.
